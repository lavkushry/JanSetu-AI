## JanSetu – Core Features

1. **Smart Location Detection** — Automatically detect the user’s approximate city or village using IP, network, and device signals without requiring GPS, with user confirmation for accuracy.

2. **Hyperlocal Discovery** — Surface nearby community discussions, events, government announcements, public programs, and essential services based on the user’s location.

3. **Public Service Requests & Tracking** — Let citizens raise complaints and service requests for civic, utility, and government services and track their progress with real-time status updates.

4. **Local Communities & Forums** — Provide Reddit-style neighborhood, village, city, and topic-based communities where citizens can discuss local issues, share information, and collaborate.

5. **Emergency & Crisis Alerts** — Deliver location-specific alerts for severe weather, natural disasters, health emergencies, infrastructure failures, and other critical situations.

6. **Verified Local Voices** — Enable verified government officials, NGOs, community leaders, organizations, and trusted contributors to publish authenticated updates and announcements.

7. **Multilingual Voice Experience** — Allow users to read, write, speak, listen, and interact with JanSetu in multiple Indian languages using speech recognition, translation, and text-to-speech.

8. **Offline-First Access** — Keep essential content and actions available in low-connectivity or offline environments and automatically synchronize changes when connectivity returns.

9. **Privacy-First Onboarding** — Minimize required personal information, clearly explain how data is used, provide granular privacy controls, and allow users to participate with appropriate levels of identity disclosure.

10. **Native Device Integration** — Support push notifications, voice commands, background synchronization, deep links, camera and document uploads, and battery-efficient device services.

11. **Location-Based Governance Feed** — Create a personalized feed of issues, projects, announcements, schemes, consultations, and government activity relevant to the user’s village, ward, district, or city.

12. **Community Issue Reporting** — Allow citizens to report potholes, garbage, water leaks, electricity problems, unsafe infrastructure, corruption concerns, and other local issues using text, images, video, voice, and location context.

13. **Issue Upvotes & Community Validation** — Let nearby users support, verify, discuss, and provide additional evidence for reported issues so widespread community problems become more visible.

14. **Government Response & Resolution Timeline** — Maintain a transparent timeline showing when an issue was reported, acknowledged, assigned, acted upon, resolved, reopened, or escalated.

15. **AI-Powered Civic Assistant** — Provide a conversational assistant that helps citizens discover schemes, understand government processes, identify the right authority, draft requests, and navigate public services.

16. **Personalized Government Scheme Discovery** — Recommend relevant central, state, district, and local government schemes based on voluntarily provided eligibility information and location.

17. **Administrative Area Mapping** — Organize communities and civic activity using India’s administrative hierarchy, including state, district, subdivision, block, municipality, ward, panchayat, and village.

18. **Reputation & Trust System** — Build contributor reputation using verified reports, constructive participation, successful resolutions, community feedback, and moderation history rather than popularity alone.

19. **AI-Assisted Moderation** — Detect spam, abuse, misinformation indicators, duplicate reports, unsafe content, and coordinated manipulation while keeping appropriate human review and appeal mechanisms.

20. **Transparent Civic Impact Dashboard** — Show citizens and administrators measurable outcomes such as reported issues, resolved cases, response times, active community concerns, service performance, and local participation.
