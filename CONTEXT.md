# JanSetu domain

JanSetu connects local social participation to accountable civic service work. Public conversation, submitted evidence, operational responsibility, and approved public progress are distinct concepts.

## Language

**Resident**:
A person participating in a locality or topic community, reading updates, or submitting a service observation.
_Avoid_: citizen when it implies a citizenship requirement, customer when it implies payment for reporting.

**Post**:
A social statement published by an author for an eligible audience, including updates, discussions, questions, and playbooks.
_Avoid_: complaint, case, official record.

**Report**:
A submitted observation with its attributed statement, time, location uncertainty, evidence, and publication preference.
_Avoid_: post, accepted obligation, verified fact.

**Case**:
A persistent operational problem connecting one or more reports to responsibility, action, evidence, and progress history.
_Avoid_: ticket when it implies one department owns the whole problem, social thread.

**Obligation**:
A defined action and outcome assigned or proposed to a responsible organization within a case.
_Avoid_: case, department ownership when several actions have different owners.

**Public receipt**:
A reviewed, sanitized public account of selected case facts and progress.
_Avoid_: raw report, reporter profile, agency acceptance receipt.

**Observation**:
The recorded relationship of a report to a case as initial, supporting, or challenging evidence.
_Avoid_: vote, automatic merge.

**Agency acknowledgement**:
An attributable agency confirmation that it received a proposed obligation.
_Avoid_: platform receipt, obligation acceptance.

**Accepted obligation**:
An attributable commitment by an authorized organization to perform a defined action.
_Avoid_: delivered, acknowledged, silence.

**Completion claim**:
An agency's assertion that assigned work has been performed, subject to the required evidence and verification.
_Avoid_: verified restoration, resolved.

**Verified restoration**:
An authorized decision that the applicable evidence and outcome requirements have been met.
_Avoid_: AI verified, work completed claim, popular opinion.

**Dispute**:
A structured disagreement about an obligation's ownership, scope, evidence, or service requirements.
_Avoid_: complaint thread, permission to stop interim safety work.

**Case coordinator**:
The operational role responsible for continuity and the next action across agency handoffs.
_Avoid_: reporter, adjudicator when no decision authority has been granted.

**Community**:
A locality or topic discussion space with membership, rules, and stewards.
_Avoid_: statutory jurisdiction, protected reporting channel.

**Usefulness vote**:
A participant's positive, negative, or withdrawn assessment of a social contribution's usefulness.
_Avoid_: evidence verification, case priority vote, duplicate like score.

**Protected report**:
A matter requiring a separately authorized reporting and specialist process whose existence and contents are excluded from public social discovery.
_Avoid_: private-community post, anonymous public allegation.

**Language readiness**:
Evidence that a particular language and product capability meet their defined release criteria.
_Avoid_: universally supported when only interface strings or one model task have been tested.
